Read before believing it

  • This is association, not cause. Students who like SACAP tend to rate everything higher. So "raise educators by one notch" means "if educators were rated like students who rate them one notch higher currently are". The real gain from fixing something is probably smaller.
  • The ratings overlap a lot. The ranking of levers is steadier than any single gain figure.
  • Who a student is barely predicts their answer once you know their ratings. Tested on held-out students, context alone scores , ratings alone , both together (pseudo R squared, cross-validated).
  • "Don't know" answers were set to the middle rating for that question: .
  • Model: multinomial logistic regression on Detractor, Passive and Promoter, weighted, fitted in scratch Python. A Turas build would use catdriver's R engine.
