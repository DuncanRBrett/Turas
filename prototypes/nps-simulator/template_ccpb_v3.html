    Read before believing it

    • This is association, not cause. Outlets that like CCPB rate everything higher. Coverage effects may partly reflect which outlets CCPB chose to serve. Real gains from fixing something are probably smaller than shown.
    • The model is ordinal (Detractor, Passive, Promoter in order), so it can move outlets down to Detractor as well as out of Promoter, even with only Detractors in the data.
    • With most outlets at 9 or 10, each rating's effect rests on the minority who scored lower. The ranges show that uncertainty.
    • Held-out fit (pseudo R squared, cross-validated): ratings only , ratings plus coverage and signage , plus size and channel controls .
    • Group dropdowns only offer combinations that exist in the data, and counts under 5 are shown as "under 5". Groups under 30 are not simulated.
    • Model fitted in scratch Python with a near-zero penalty. A Turas build would use catdriver's R engine.
